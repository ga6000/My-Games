Magnetile Drop

Drag a tile out of the tray and let go over the floor. Flick while letting go to throw it.
While holding (each press turns 45°, hold Shift for 15°): WS tilt away / toward, AD spin left / right, QE roll left / right, R stand on edge, F lay flat, wheel raises / lowers, Space drop, Esc put back. A held tile floats and gets tugged onto nearby magnet edges.
1–5 grab a slot without dragging (click to drop). Click a tile on the floor to pick it up again.
←→ rotate view, wheel zooms (when not holding), M magnets, C clear.
Click anywhere to start.
