# Workshop round 1 — Zombie Arcade + Space Tracer (2026-10-09)

The first run of `hub-visual-review` v0.1 and `hub-gameplay-review` v0.1. These are findings, not
plans. The point of the round is to judge the **skills** as much as the games: see "What the

## A. Gameplay — Zombie Arcade

| # | Pillar | Applies? | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instant start | Yes | **1** | 11.4s intro (`ARC_INTRO_MS`), then first contact ~13s. It **replays on every restart**: `resetGame` clears `gameStarted`, so `startGame` runs `arcadeBeginIntro` again |
| 2 | One-sentence controls | Yes | 2 | "Move, aim, shoot, F at the tank." Plus 1–7 / wheel to switch guns and F for gun/perk drops: about 6 things to know |
| 3 | Fast death, instant restart | Yes | 1 | The death is readable (the red edges are the two-step buffer). But the card types its readout for ~8s, and a restart replays the 11.4s intro: ~20s from death to playing |
| 4 | Building pressure | Yes | **3** | Spawn rate doubles every 50s, level every 12s, overwhelm ~4:00. It defines the game |
| 5 | Tension / release | Yes | 1 | Three tank flips are the beats, but a flip is a generator sound only: no fanfare, no lull. The swarm never breathes |

| 17 | Own core action | Yes | **0** | See §C: shares camera + body + verbs + threat with RD Arena |

**Total 30 / 51 over 17 applicable pillars.**

**Arcade gate:** a session fits 2–4 min ✔ · pillars 1/3/4 ≥2 ✘ (1 and 3 both score 1) · score a
better player reliably beats ✔ (13 at 2) · no menu between runs ✔ → **ARCADE-ADJACENT (missing:
instant start and instant restart).** Both are pacing, not design. This is the cheapest gate to
pass on the board.

**Commercial lineage:** *Robotron 2084* (the swarm), *CoD Zombies* (perks, co-op, the escape),
*Left 4 Dead* (finale-then-leave). **The sentence:** "In Zombie Arcade the horde is your fuel:
you have to kill them *on the funnels* to power your own way out." Neither Robotron nor CoD makes
where you kill the objective. **Distinct, and the funnel is the hook, so it should be louder.**

**Hole poked:** pillar 16 (co-op) and pillar 13 (a personal score) pull against each other. Per
player, `runScoreFor` makes kills a competition between teammates, and the one-taker drops
sharpen it. That's fine if the rivalry is the fun. It's wrong if the group plays this as a team
game. **Your call: is Zombie Arcade a team score or a personal score?**

**Top 3 by pillar gain per effort:**
1. **Intro only on the first run of a sitting; a restart drops straight in, and the readout can be
   skipped by clicking.** Pillars 1 and 3: 1 → 2–3 each, and it passes the gate. Small.
2. **A tank-flip beat.** A short fanfare, a 3–4s spawn pause, and a "TANK 2 OPEN" call. Pillar 5:
   1 → 2. Small.
3. **A funnel-kill multiplier.** Kills on a live funnel count ×2 while the tank fills. Pillars 10
   and 11, and it makes the hook louder. Small to medium.

**Needs a playtest to judge:** 7, 14, 15.

## B. Gameplay — Space Tracer

| # | Pillar | Applies? | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instant start | Yes | 1 | Click → name + room form → "PRESS TO ENTER GAME": three screens before flying |
| 2 | One-sentence controls | Yes | 3 | "Steer with the mouse, click to shoot, hold SPACE to charge a dash." |
| 3 | Fast death, instant restart | Yes | 2 | Respawn at the safe zone, the life score resets. Fast. Border deaths may read as unfair: playtest needed |
| 4 | Building pressure | Yes | **0** | Asteroids hold at ~12 (1-for-1 respawn). Nothing escalates within a life. Pressure exists only if other players are in the room |
| 5 | Tension / release | Yes | 0 | No waves, no objectives. One continuous state |
| 6 | Clock | Partly | 0 | No timer. The lethal border is a space constraint, not a clock |
| 7 | Readable chaos | Yes | 2 | Vector ships in identity colours, magenta border, cyan safe zone. Rarely chaotic enough to test |
| 8 | Few types, one verb each | Yes | 1 | Asteroids (drift) and players. One enemy type in solo play |
| 9 | Off-screen awareness | Yes | 0 | Hard-follow camera, no radar. Rivals arrive unannounced |
| 10 | Greed vs safety | Yes | 1 | The streak multiplier is greed (keep killing, never die), but there's no place on the map where the points are |
| 11 | Score scales with skill | Yes | **3** | The ×0.1-per-kill uncapped streak, plus time and distance. The best scoring design on the board |
| 12 | Emergency button | Yes | 2 | The dash: charge up to 10s, burst up to 5s. Right to save, right to spend |
| 13 | Public score target | Yes | 2 | A room BEST board, the leaderboard. Not 1UP / HI-SCORE, not zero-padded |
| 14 | Sound and voice | Yes | 2 | Sample-based thwops and music. No danger voice |
| 15 | Worth mastering | Yes | 2 | Momentum steering, dash timing, the beam's ricochet |
| 16 | Shared moments | Yes | 2 | Free-for-all kills and streak-ending. Nothing co-op, nothing that builds to a moment |
| 17 | Own core action | Yes | **3** | The only ship and the only momentum steering on the board |

**Total 25 / 51 over 17 applicable pillars.** Pillar 6 is scored as applying, since a match
timer would make it apply.

**Arcade gate:** session fits ✘ (no end, it's a drop-in arena) · 1/3/4 ≥2 ✘ (1 and 4) · score
beats ✔ · no menu between runs ✔ → **NOT ARCADE as built: an .io-style drop-in arena** (genre:
*slither.io / diep.io* with *Asteroids* physics). Each **life** is arcade-shaped. The **session**
isn't.

**Commercial lineage:** *Asteroids*, *Spacewar!*, *diep.io*. **The sentence:** "In Space Tracer
you ___, which Asteroids never lets you do." The honest fill is "fight your friends", and
*Spacewar!* (1962) did that. **Not conceptually distinct yet.** The trail is drawn but does
nothing. If the trail *mattered* (a wall, a hazard, territory), that would be the sentence: the
name already promises it.

**Hole poked:** is this a cabinet at all, or the hub's lobby toy? It's the one game the group can
drop into at any time with no session, which is valuable, but it is the opposite of a 2–5 minute
cabinet. Either give it a match (3 minutes, a winner card), or name it the lobby game and stop
measuring it as a cabinet.

**Top 3 by pillar gain per effort:**
1. **A 3-minute match with a winner card and a ramp.** The asteroid count and speed climb over the
   match, then a final 30s where the border shrinks. Pillars 4, 5, 6 and 13, and it passes the
   gate. Medium.
2. **One screen before flying.** The name defaults to the hub's identity; the room comes from the
   hub link. Pillar 1: 1 → 3. Small.
3. **The trail as a mechanic.** For example, rival trails are lethal for 2s after they're drawn
   (light-cycle in space). Pillars 17 and 10, and the distinctness sentence. Medium, and a design
   call: yours.

**Needs a playtest to judge:** 3 (border deaths), 15.

## C. Hub fingerprint (the board, plus Reality Rewrite as asked)

| | Camera | Body | Verbs | Threat | Objective | Structure | Social | Upgrades | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Zombie Arcade** | top-down follow | on foot | move + shoot | AI swarm | fill 3 tanks, escape | single run | co-op | drops, perks | kills on funnels as fuel |
| **RD Arena** | top-down follow | on foot | move + shoot (+ build) | AI + growing flesh field | defend sanctuaries and organs | rounds | co-op | cards | a living reaction-diffusion terrain |
| **Reality Rewrite** *(shelved)* | top-down | on foot (fighter) | move + shoot | players | outlast, rank | rounds + author phase | versus | rule cards | rewriting the rules between rounds |
| **Space Tracer** | top-down follow | ship | steer + shoot + dash | players + asteroids | score per life | endless lives | free-for-all | none | momentum + streak multiplier |
| **Glass City Escape** | top-down | on foot | move + carry | drones, race | carry cores to pedestals | levels | race (ghosts) | none | building-hopping |
| **4D Pong** | fixed screen | paddle | move + stun | players / AI | tug-of-war | match | versus 2–4 | power-up | four-sided tug-of-war |

**The overlap rule fires once on the board:** **Zombie Arcade × RD Arena** share camera, body,
verbs and threat. To a player sitting down cold, both are "top-down, on foot, shoot the
monsters, co-op". Their hooks (funnel fuel vs living flesh terrain) are real, but neither shows
in the first 10 seconds.

**Reality Rewrite** shares camera + body + verbs with both, differing only in threat (PvP). If it
were promoted, the board would have three top-down on-foot shooters out of five.

**Your question: "are these really arcade games?"** Zombie: nearly (pacing only). RD Arena: not
reviewed yet; its rounds and card offers point to *roguelite* rather than arcade. Reality
Rewrite: a *party brawler* with a card-drafting meta. Arcade-adjacent at best.

**Resolutions to choose from (yours):**
- **Differentiate RD Arena by moving its verb.** Make it the *builder*: no gun, you place
  antibodies and walls into the flesh and steer the growth (closer to *Qix* or a tower-defence
  cabinet). Zombie keeps the gun. This uses what RD Arena already has (a build state in
  `rd-input.js`).
- **Differentiate by camera.** One goes fixed-screen single-arena (Robotron proper) and the other
  stays scrolling. A cheaper split, but a weaker one.
- **Leave Reality Rewrite shelved** until one of the two is moved off the shooter verb, or promote
  it only as RD Arena's replacement.

## What the round taught the skills

1. **Gameplay pillar 17 duplicates §3b.** Scoring it 0–3 by feel and then again in the table was
   double work. Keep it, but its score should come *from* the fingerprint table: 0 = full overlap,
   3 = unique on four fields.
2. **"Applies?" needs a rule for "not built yet."** Space Tracer has no clock, but could. Proposed
   rule: score a pillar as applying if the game's own premise could hold it. Mark "No" only when
   the premise rules it out (4D Pong and a radar).
3. **The gate needs a "session" vs "life" distinction.** Space Tracer's lives are arcade-shaped
   and its session isn't. The skill should ask about both.
4. **Visual review must check the ground, not just tokens.** Zombie's biggest deviation (a lit
   green world on a "pure black" system) was never in any checklist item. Promote §2.1 to an
   explicit "what fills most of the screen" check.
5. **Visual review should flag naming and title consistency** (Gyro Space / Space Tracer). That's
   a cohesion problem the checklist didn't ask about. It may belong to lore-check later.
6. **Pending direction decisions block everything downstream.** Both skills should surface an
   undecided direction (FAITH) as the first line, not as one clash among many.
