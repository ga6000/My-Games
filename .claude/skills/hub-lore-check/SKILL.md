## The premise (from the user, 2026-10-09)

All the hub games, and the hub itself, take place **in one universe**, at different locations,
with different characters. Together they form one **loose, implied story**. Implied, not told:
the connections should be discoverable, never explained in a cutscene.

## 1. Canon — TO BE WRITTEN BY THE USER

- **The world:** _(what happened, when, the tone)_
- **Locations:** _(one per game; where the hub itself is)_
- **Factions and people:** _(who exists, who is never shown)_
- **Creatures:** _(what they are, where they came from, what they can and can't do)_
- **Technology level:** _(what exists; what would break the world if it appeared)_
- **The thread:** _(what links the games; what a player pieces together)_
- **Naming and voice:** _(how places, enemies and on-screen text sound)_
- **Hard nos:** _(things that must never appear)_

## 2. What a check covers (once canon exists)

For the game under review, list each item and mark it **FITS / STRETCH (why) / BREAKS (which
canon line)**:
- characters and player roles (Zombie's MEDIC/ENGINEER/SCOUT/GUNNER, for example)
- creatures and enemies (names, origins as implied by their look and behaviour)
- the setting and its props
- implied technology (weapons, power, vehicles, computers)
- on-screen text: titles, intros, objectives, end cards, item and perk names
- connections: does the game leave a thread for another card, and is it the right one?

Output:
```
LORE CHECK — <game>
Verdict: PASS | PASS WITH STRETCHES | FAIL
Breaks: <item → canon line>
Stretches: <item → why>
Threads to other games: <present / missing / suggested (→ inbox)>
```

## 3. Inventory (works without canon)

Collect, per game, everything the player can read or infer: names, enemy types, the setting, tech,
text strings. This is the raw material the user's canon will be checked against, and it is useful
to show them before they write it. Keep it to one table per game.

## 4. Questions to ask the user while canon is empty

1. What happened to this world, and roughly when?
2. Where is each board game set, and where does the hub sit (an arcade inside the world? an
   outside frame)?
3. What are the creatures in Zombie and the flesh in RD Arena: the same thing, or different?
4. What technology level is canon: Space Tracer's ships and Glass City's drones, against Zombie's
   1980s-feeling warehouse?
5. What must never appear?
