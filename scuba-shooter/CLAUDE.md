# Scuba Shooter

`scuba-shooter.html` (fonts inlined), plus the L+G dev panel: `../shared/devtools.js` loads first and `scuba-dev.js` registers last (both guarded, so the page runs without them). Built 2026-11-01/02 in a Claude project thread from the Gemini "Deep Ocean Exploration" prototype. Promoted to the hub board 2026-11-02 at Ry's request (solo, stamped SOLO).

- **Loop:** an intro flyover (Space skips), drop from a hovering helicopter, pop the 10 green specimens without dying, then ESCAPE: the crab-snake erupts from the seabed and hunts you while you swim to the helicopter's ladder.
- **Intro (2026-11-02):** one zoomed-out (`viewZ` = `INTRO_Z` 0.34) Catmull-Rom loop of camera centres (down the left, along the seabed bases, up the right, back across the surface), trapezoid-eased, always in inside view, then zooms in to play scale on the diver. `viewZ` works by running `drawWorldPass` with a logical viewport of width/viewZ.
- **Finale:** the last pop calls `startFinale`: freeze + fade to black, cut to the seabed tube nearest x 6000 (`finale.bx`), the crab-snake bursts out (`snake.mode = 'burst'`), crates fly up and float to the surface (`crates`), cut back to the diver with an ESCAPE banner, `startExtraction()`. During the escape `snake.hunt`: it chases (flow field when blocked), can breach ~80px above the surface with momentum, its mouth and tendril grabs call `damagePlayer`, and shooting tendril sections still severs them.
- **Music:** `updateMusic` (synth): D drone, Phrygian pad notes through a feedback delay, bells in the deep; darker with depth, muffled inside, a heartbeat and minor-second cluster under threat, a driving pulse during the escape. Silent in the attract run.
- **Attract run (2026-11-02):** like Zombie's `zDemo`. On load, a god-tier AI diver (`demoUpdate`, drives the real `update()` through `keys`/mouse) holds off an endless flow of creatures in open water with a belt-fed underwater machine gun (pistol with cd 3, no reload, `drawMachineGun`) and the odd harpoon + stock bash. It never dies (`damagePlayer`, `hitSpecimen`, `endGame` are no-ops while `window.__demoActive`). Any click or key sets sessionStorage `scubaDive` and reloads into the real run (intro sweep, helicopter). `?nodemo=1` turns it off; the headless tests use it. The attract run also has the **crab-snake** (`makeSnake`/`updateSnake`/`drawSnake`, attract only, never in normal play): 24 rotting armoured segments, 2x a rig column thick (`SN_R = LEG_W`), roaming `SN_BOX` right of the diver, invulnerable. 9 tendril pairs (FABRIK chains, `TJ` joints) crawl, reach for the diver inside a cone (`T_CONE`) and their length, and grab/haul him in; `SEG_HP` hits on a tendril section break it (`breakSegment`) and sever the pair, which regrows after `REGROW` frames. The god diver only fires at targets inside `RANGE_WATER`; the attract camera frames diver + snake. The diver dodges by scoring 16 headings against predicted creature positions, the snake, rock/rig and the home loop; swarms arrive in batches (`demoBatch`) from a rotating direction, every 4th from all sides.
- **End:** a Zombie-style typed score readout (`renderRunScore`): specimens popped ×5,000, NPCs alive ×2,000, accuracy ×20,000 (hits/shots on creatures and specimens, `shotStats`), and on a win time bonus (300 − seconds) ×100 plus 25,000 for extracting. Click or Enter returns to `../index.html`.
- **Style:** RASTER only (the original palette was removed 2026-11-02): renders at 1/3 resolution through an SVG posterize filter.
- **Systems:** one walk/swim controller over floor segments (`seg`), a floor nav graph (`buildNav`/`navPath`) for bots and creatures, a 50px water flow field for swimmers, crossings at moonpools/airlocks/ladders (`entries`). Zombie wound rules with a magenta edge (`damagePlayer`). GORE 3 blood baked into decal tiles. 14 creatures (`CK` kinds tank/lanky/flood, speeds ×1.3 on 2026-11-02).
- **Session length:** not yet tuned to SCOPE_RULES' 2–5 minutes, and not playtested with the group.
- **Dev panel (L+G, 2026-11-02):** `scuba-dev.js`. Objectives (skip intro, pop next / go to next specimen, leave one, pop all → finale, skip to escape, call heli, win, die), spawns (tank, lanky, flood, swarm, kill/clear all), crab-snake (release hunting, burst cutscene, freeze, sever/regrow tendrils, remove), player (god, heal, infinite ammo, teleports), clear clutter, restart. Sliders: game speed, creature speed (rescales `CK`), snake speed, darkness, gore (`GORE_MUL`), music volume. The game reads its knobs from `DEV` (= `window.SCUBA_DEV`). Pauses the game while open unless RUN BEHIND PANEL. The attract's any-key handler ignores L and G so the combo works there.
- **Hub link:** the Esc menu's Return to hub and the end card both point at `../index.html`.
- **Hub session (2026-11-13):** opened from the board (`hub=1`) it skips the attract run, connects `mp-core` only to report, and hands its run score to `PARTY.finish()` (on click or Enter, or 9s after the end card in a session so nobody waits). Opened directly, nothing changed.
